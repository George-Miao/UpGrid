# Deliver alerts at least once through a replicated outbox

An availability state transition and its pending alerts enter Raft state atomically, after which only the leader delivers the replicated outbox with bounded exponential backoff. Transport failures, `408`, `429`, and responses with a valid `Retry-After` are retried for up to 24 hours; every other HTTP response, including an unqualified `5xx`, is terminal. Delivery is at least once: a leader crash after an external service accepts an alert but before Raft records success may cause a duplicate. Webhook payloads therefore carry a stable alert ID that receivers can use for deduplication.
